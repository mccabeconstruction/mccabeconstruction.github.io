Introduction


Organization and transparency are what McCabe Construction run on. We understand that a well laid out plan and clear open communication with clients and trade partners can often be the difference between a successful project and one where the costs and schedules can spiral.

Whether its creating a new space or improving a current one, we are passionate about both the form and function of a build knowing that one without the other leaves a lot to be desired. With open conversation about the intended purpose and priorities of the project from the start we are able to curate a scope that fits your needs.

About Me


Hi, I’m Toby, owner operator of McCabe Construction. I grew up in England wanting to be an accountant then immigrated to Canada in 2010 to be with my then girlfriend, now wife Carleigh. Like many immigrants it was there where I really started my journey into the trades working for various companies from tiling, to drywall, to framing and finish carpentry. I noticed fairly quickly that what I really enjoyed about projects was seeing the entirety of the construction progress from the very start to the hand over day.
Moving from Saskatoon to Kelowna in 2017 I narrowed in more on residential renovations where I excelled in my roles as lead hand for a prestigious award winning builder working on high end homes. It was there where I honed my skills and found new appreciation for what custom meant in an industry that is so often cookie cutter.
After soaking in as much knowledge as I could, I finally started McCabe Construction in 2021 and have been enjoying making clients wishes a reality ever since :)
